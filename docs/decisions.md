# Analysis decisions

Choices made after the ISMRM 2027 abstract (tag `ismrm2027-abstract`), with the alternative we did not take, so either can be revisited. Evidence is in the linked issues (current reconstructions, QSMxT v10.0.0).

| # | Decision | Chosen | Alternative | To switch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Test-retest metric ([#2](https://github.com/astewartau/emtp-travelling-heads/issues/2)) | Median \|a − 1\| stays primary for both test-retest and inter-scanner pairs, so the two are directly comparable and match QSM-CI. SD of a is reported as a secondary test-retest measure | SD of a as the primary test-retest measure: no systematic scaling is expected within a scanner and protocol, so its spread is the natural measure. The two rank the protocols the same way | `numbers.json` already has both (`median_sd_slope_pct`); in Fig. 3B, plot the per-pipeline SD of `slope` over test-retest pairs (as computed in `abstract_numbers.py`) instead of median \|a − 1\| |
| D2 | Bridge test-retest and the first run ([#10](https://github.com/astewartau/emtp-travelling-heads/issues/10)) | Report bridge test-retest with and without pairs involving run 1 (the first scan of every session, the odd run in 9 of 12 sessions) | Report all pairs only, as in the abstract, or drop run 1 from all analyses | `numbers.json`: `median_abs_slope_dev_without_run1_pct` |
| D3 | Limits of agreement ([#3](https://github.com/astewartau/emtp-travelling-heads/issues/3)) | Secondary measure: LoA after correcting each pair for its fitted scaling (`ba_loa_corrected_ppm`); uncorrected LoA in the supplement | Uncorrected Bland-Altman LoA as the secondary measure (as in the abstract). The two differ by 5–8% | Both are in `per_pipeline.csv` and `numbers.json` |
| D4 | Fig. 4 bars ([#4](https://github.com/astewartau/emtp-travelling-heads/issues/4)) | Uncorrected bars, with the definition of each bar in the caption and notebook. Corrected bars only once they can be estimated out of sample (after the five pending sessions, #1/#5) | Show scaling-corrected protocol and scanner bars now. They are in-sample (the transform is estimated on the scans it corrects), so they overstate what calibration achieves | `followup.py`: `protocol_spread_corrected`, `scanner_spread_corrected` |
| D5 | Unit of comparison ([#7](https://github.com/astewartau/emtp-travelling-heads/issues/7)) | The scanner, with its local protocol, is the unit everywhere; the six locations are "institutions". Site 1 noted as the only institution whose two scanners share a local protocol (`data/protocols.csv`) | Keep "site" as the unit and report intra-/inter-site comparisons (one intra-site pair exists, GE vs Siemens at site 5, and it agrees no better than inter-site pairs) | Wording in text and Fig. 1B; IDs (`site<N>-<model>`) are unchanged either way |
| D6 | Next acquisitions ([#9](https://github.com/astewartau/emtp-travelling-heads/issues/9), [#12](https://github.com/astewartau/emtp-travelling-heads/issues/12)) | In order: (1) a dummy GRE before the first bridge run, or randomised run order; (2) local protocols that differ substantially from the bridge (SWI-style anisotropic single-echo, UK Biobank-like thick slices, 3D-EPI) on the same subjects at a few sites; (3) one repositioned bridge rescan per session on the same scanner, ideally with varied head pitch | 2D GRE (used by some longitudinal cohorts) in addition; or no new protocols, focusing on pipelines (variation between acquisition protocols is small compared with vendor and pipeline) | A group decision; this is a proposal |
